I don't understand recursion.
S
AI
No problem! Let's understand it using a simple example.

Imagine you're standing in a line of people, and you ask the person in front of you: "What position am I in?" They don't know either, so they ask the person in front of them... and so on until someone at the front says "I'm first!"

That's recursion — a function that calls itself until it hits a base case. 😊
Oh, that makes sense! What's a base case?
S
AI
Great question! The base case is the condition that stops the recursion. In our example, it's the person at the front saying "I'm first!" — that's when the answers start coming back. Without a base case, the function would call itself forever! 💡
